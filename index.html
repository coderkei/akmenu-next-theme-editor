How to use the preview

Clicks: Move things lets you click or tap something, like the clock or a button, to move it. Drag the round handle (or use the arrow keys; Shift moves 8 pixels). Things with a width and height also get a corner handle to resize them. Press the tick, or just click the next thing you want to move; Esc puts it back. The game list still responds to taps. To move the start menu, open it with the START button below the screens (or Space), then click it.

Clicks: Use the menu makes the bottom screen a touchscreen, just like the DS: every button can be tapped.

In either mode you can use the buttons under the screens, or click the screens and use the keyboard: arrows, X or Enter for A, Z or Esc for B, S for X, A for Y, Q and W for L and R, Space for START, Shift for SELECT.

It’s drawn the way AKMenu-Next draws it. Game icons, covers and your DS name are stand-ins.
